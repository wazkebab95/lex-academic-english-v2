About the Practice

Language with purpose.
Learning with precision.

Fex Academic English is an independent language and linguistics practice led by Fawwaz Izeddin. It brings together academic English instruction, specialist communication training and linguistic services, with a focus on helping people use language accurately, confidently and effectively in the settings that matter to them.

The Person Behind the Practice

A linguist's perspective, applied to real communication.

Fawwaz Izeddin is a linguist, language educator, translator and interpreter whose work spans English language teaching, academic communication and multilingual services. His experience includes supporting learners with different professional and educational goals, as well as working with language across institutional, commercial and cross-cultural settings.

This combination informs the practice: language is not treated as a collection of rules to memorise, but as a practical and intellectual skill. Good communication requires control of grammar and vocabulary, but also judgement, register, structure, audience awareness and the ability to make meaning clear.

Teaching and consultation are delivered online to individuals and organisations internationally. Each engagement is shaped around the learner's or client's objectives rather than a fixed, one-size-fits-all package.

Our Approach

Rigour, relevance and individual attention.

The aim is not simply to complete lessons or accumulate vocabulary. It is to build durable competence that learners can transfer to examinations, university study and professional life.

01 · Diagnose

Understand the starting point

Identify existing strengths, recurring language patterns and the specific demands of the learner's or organisation's goal.

02 · Develop

Teach with structure

Combine explicit instruction, guided practice and purposeful feedback so that progress is understood and applied.

03 · Apply

Transfer learning to practice

Work with authentic tasks and relevant scenarios, helping learners communicate independently beyond the lesson.
