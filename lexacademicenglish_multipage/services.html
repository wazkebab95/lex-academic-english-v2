Programme Structure

Frequently Asked Questions

How are lessons and diagnostic exercises structured? +

Every student begins with a baseline diagnostic evaluating grammatical range, lexical precision, and discourse coherence. Between live sessions, enrolled learners complete targeted drills inside the Student Portal with automated British English feedback reports.

Do you offer both individual and corporate group packages? +

Yes. Individual programmes are tailored around your target exam date or conference deadline, while corporate and retail modules can be scheduled for small departmental cohorts.

Which spelling and register standards do you teach? +

All academic curricula, diagnostic materials, and editorial reviews follow formal British English conventions while preparing learners for international academic and commercial contexts.
