Module 04 · Applied workplace task

The seasonal stock shortfall

Analyse the facts, propose a practical response and review your own communication.

Business situation

A retailer is preparing a weekend promotion across two stores and its online shop. A supplier delivery has arrived incomplete. The promotion begins in four days, and the operations team needs a realistic recovery plan.

600Units ordered
420Units received
180Units missing
4 daysUntil promotion launch

Known constraints

  • The supplier has not yet confirmed the reason for the shortfall.
  • A split delivery may be possible within 48 hours, but expedited freight has an additional cost.
  • Both stores and the online team need accurate availability information.
  • No one has yet authorised extra freight expenditure.

Do not assume that the supplier, store team or budget owner has approved a particular solution. Identify what must be confirmed.

Your response

Privacy: written responses remain in this page session only. They are not submitted, stored or sent to your tutor. Copy your work before leaving if you need to keep it.

Self-review checklist

View a model approach

Priorities: Reconcile the purchase order, delivery note and physical count; confirm which SKUs and sizes are missing; check stock at both stores and the fulfilment location; then assess customer and promotion impact.

Supplier message: “We have received 420 of the 600 units on the purchase order. Please confirm the reason for the 180-unit shortfall and whether a split delivery can reach us within 48 hours. Kindly provide the proposed quantities, dispatch time, freight cost and revised delivery schedule by 12:00 tomorrow. We will review any additional cost with the relevant budget owner before approval.”

Internal update: “The delivery is 180 units short. We are verifying the SKU-level impact and have requested a recovery proposal from the supplier. Please avoid confirming availability for affected lines until the stock position is reconciled. I will share the next update by 14:00.”

This is one possible approach. A good answer should fit the learner’s actual authority, company policy and operational context.
