SHEET 01 · IELTS TASK 1DATA COMMENTARY & PROPORTIONS

Upgrading Mechanical Trend Descriptions

Band 6.0 MechanicalBand 8.0+ PrecisionGrammatical Pattern
Solar power went up very fast from 12% to 38%. Solar generation more than tripled its share, surging from 12% to 38%. Proportional multiplier + participle clause
Coal fell down to 6% and stayed the same. Coal reliance contracted precipitously to 6% before plateauing. C1 verb + adverb + temporal preposition
Germany had the highest number at 45%. Germany accounted for the largest proportion, peaking at 45%. Domain verb (account for) + non-finite clause
SHEET 02 · IELTS TASK 2HEDGING & ACADEMIC DISTANCE

Replacing Sweeping Generalisations

Overgeneralised ClaimAcademically Hedged Claim
Remote work always makes employees less productive. Unstructured remote work tends to correlate with diminished team cohesion in collaborative sectors.
Governments must ban private cars in cities immediately. Municipal authorities ought to consider phased restrictions on private vehicles in congested urban cores.
Many people think tuition fees are unfair. It is widely contended that escalating tuition fees exacerbate socioeconomic inequality.
